Organisation and Transformation
Advisory
Organisations are living systems. Structure, culture, leadership, relationships and strategy shape
one another, often in ways that are not immediately visible.
TOD works with leaders to understand what is happening beneath the surface, identify the few
issues that matter most and design practical change that fits the organisation’s context. We
combine rigorous diagnosis with close partnership, helping clients move from insight to alignment
and sustained action.
Typical client situations
• A strategy is clear, but execution remains fragmented.
 
• Growth or restructuring has created unclear roles and decision rights.
 
• A leadership team needs stronger alignment and collective accountability.
 
• Culture is not supporting the organisation’s ambition.
 
• A family business needs to prepare for governance or succession.
 
• A transformation requires stronger ownership, communication and adoption.
 
How we engage
We begin with discovery. We listen to the people closest to the work, examine the formal and
informal system, and agree what success should look like. The engagement may combine diagnosis,
facilitated alignment, organisation design, leadership work, change support and ongoing advisory.
Action: Discuss Your Organisation
Leadership Coaching and Mentoring
Leadership develops through experience, reflection, challenge and application. Our journeys help

About TOD
Total Organization Development is a Saudi-based organisational development and leadership
consultancy. We bring together organisational advisory, leadership development, executive
coaching, mentoring and learning design to help organisations build capability that lasts.
Our work begins with context. We seek to understand the organisation as it is: its ambition,
pressures, relationships, strengths and patterns. From there, we work alongside leaders to design
development and change that is practical, relevant and owned by the people who will carry it
forward.
Human Systems
Human Systems is more than a strapline. It reflects how we see organisations: not as charts,
processes or isolated individuals, but as living networks of people, purpose, relationships, decisions
and behaviour. Sustainable change happens when these elements are understood and developed
together.

Case studies and proof
Credibility should come from specific approved evidence. Make it clear whether a claim belongs to
TOD, the current leadership team, an associate or a delivery partner.
Case study structure
• Context: the organisational situation and audience.
 
• Challenge: what the client needed to address.
 
• Approach: what TOD or its leadership designed and delivered.
 
• Scale: duration, cohort size or delivery footprint where approved.
 
• Outcome: observable result, participant evidence or client measure.
 
• Confidentiality: anonymise the organisation where permission is unavailable.

Developer implementation brief
Content structure
• Use Services as the main navigation label, with three dedicated service pages beneath it.
 
• Use one primary action across the site: Start a Conversation.
 
• Build client logos, case studies, team profiles and insights as reusable content types.
 
• Launch with strong core pages; add insights and case studies through repeatable templates.
 
• Prepare paired English and Arabic fields and right-to-left layouts from the beginning.
 
• Do not publish “since”, client-count or participant-count claims until TOD confirms the evidence.
 
• Avoid TOD Institute as a public label unless it is confirmed as appropriate for TOD’s registration.
 
• Keep the corporate website and mentoring platform technically separate but visually related.
 
Mentoring platform
Within Leadership, Coaching and Mentoring, explain that TOD can combine mentoring strategy,
mentor development, programme governance and digital enablement. Link to the platform’s
separate domain only after the product name, ownership, privacy model and client configuration are
approved.
